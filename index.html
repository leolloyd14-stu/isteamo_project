Stress Management Techniques for School

  • 📚 Break assignments into smaller tasks to reduce overwhelm.
  • 🧘 Take 5-minute breathing breaks between study sessions.
  • ⏰ Use a timetable to organise homework and deadlines.
  • 💧 Stay hydrated and eat healthy meals during busy school days.
  • 😴 Get enough sleep before exams for better concentration.
  • 🚶 Take short walks after class to clear your mind.
  • 🤝 Talk to teachers, friends or family when feeling stressed.
