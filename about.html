About Calm Garden

A student-focused stress management website built to make emotional wellbeing feel simple, visual, and encouraging.

Our purpose

Helping students stress less and grow more.

Calm Garden helps students understand their emotions, manage school pressure, and build healthier habits through calming activities, reflection, AI support, and a virtual plant.

Our Purpose

The main goal of Calm Garden is to make stress management simple, visual, and encouraging. Instead of only reading about stress, users can interact with a virtual plant that represents their mental wellbeing. When users practise healthy coping techniques, their plant becomes healthier, showing progress in a calm and motivating way.

How Calm Garden Helps You

Wellbeing tools arranged in a friendly dashboard experience.

😌

Understand Your Feelings

Daily check-ins help users recognise their stress levels and become more aware of their emotions.

🌬️

Calm Your Mind

Breathing exercises and relaxation tools help reduce tension, anxiety, and school-related stress.

📓

🌱

Build Healthy Habits

By practising regularly, users develop better routines for managing pressure in school and daily life.

Inspiration Behind This Project

Calm Garden was inspired by the idea that mental health can grow like a plant. Just as plants need water, sunlight, and care, our minds need rest, support, and healthy habits. This project uses nature, soft colours, and simple psychology-based activities to make stress management feel less overwhelming and more enjoyable.
