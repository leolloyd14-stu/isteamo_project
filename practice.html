Quick Relaxation Activities

  • 🧘 Take 5 slow breaths before starting homework.
  • 🚶 Go for a short walk after a stressful lesson.
  • 💧 Drink water and stretch your body for 2 minutes.
  • 👀 Look around and name 5 things you can see to ground yourself.
  • 🤗 Speak kindly to yourself: “I am trying my best.”
