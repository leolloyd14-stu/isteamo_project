Calming Sounds

🌧️ Rain Sounds

Gentle rain can help you relax and focus while studying.

🌊 Ocean Waves

Ocean sounds can create a peaceful feeling and reduce tension.

🌲 Forest Sounds

Forest sounds can make your mind feel calm and connected to nature.

Stress Journal

Write down what is causing your stress and how you are feeling. This can help you understand your emotions more clearly.


Quick Relaxation Activities

  • 🧘 Take 5 slow breaths before starting homework.
  • 🚶 Go for a short walk after a stressful lesson.
  • 💧 Drink water and stretch your body for 2 minutes.
  • 👀 Look around and name 5 things you can see to ground yourself.
  • 🤗 Speak kindly to yourself: “I am trying my best.”
